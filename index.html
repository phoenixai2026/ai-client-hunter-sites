FAQ

A few things you might be wondering.

Who owns my domain?

You do. We recommend that your domain is registered in your own name so you remain in control of it.

Do I have to sign up for Website Care?

No. Website Care is optional. It's available for businesses that would prefer us to handle hosting, SSL, technical maintenance and minor updates.

Can I request changes to the website?

Yes. Website Setup includes two rounds of revisions before launch. Larger additions, new pages or integrations can be quoted separately.

How long does it take?

Timing depends on the size of the website and how quickly we receive your business information, images and feedback.

Can you use my existing logo and photos?

Absolutely. If you have your own logo, photos and branding, we can use them to make the website feel more like your business.

Ready to get your business online?

Tell us a little about your business and what you're looking for. We'll get back to you to discuss your website.
