Privacy Policy.

How we collect, use and handle information when you work with WebLorosae.

Version 1.0Effective 17 September 2026
01

About this policy

WebLorosae provides website design, development and optional Website Care services to Australian small businesses. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, contact us, complete our onboarding form or purchase our services.

Website: weblorosae.com.au
Contact: hello@weblorosae.com.au

02

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, telephone number and business contact details.
  • Information about your business, services, service areas, operating hours and website preferences.
  • Logos, photographs and other files you provide for your website.
  • Your Website Care selection and records of your acceptance of our Terms of Service.
  • Payment status, transaction references and details needed to administer your purchase.
  • Information you provide when contacting us for enquiries or support.

We may also collect publicly available business contact information when researching prospective customers. Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless we specifically request it.

03

How we collect information

We collect information when you contact us, complete our onboarding form, upload files, purchase services or request support. We may also obtain business contact details from publicly available business websites and directories.

04

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and communicate with customers.
  • Prepare, create, launch and support websites.
  • Organise customer-supplied project materials.
  • Process payments, administer optional Website Care and maintain transaction records.
  • Record customer instructions and acceptance of our Terms of Service.
  • Maintain business records and meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Contact prospective businesses where permitted by applicable law.

Agreeing to our Terms of Service does not automatically mean you agree to receive unrelated marketing.

05

Payments and Website Care

We use Stripe to provide online checkout and process payments. You enter payment card details through Stripe Checkout. WebLorosae receives payment-related details needed to confirm purchases and administer services, such as payment status and transaction references.

If you select optional Website Care, Stripe may retain a payment method for future charges under the payment arrangement you agree to. You can read more in Stripe’s privacy policy.

06

Third-party services

We use third-party services to operate our business:

  • n8n: onboarding forms and workflow automation.
  • Google Drive: customer files and website project materials.
  • Google Sheets: onboarding, project and payment records.
  • Stripe: checkout, payment processing and billing.

These providers may process information as part of delivering their services. We may also share information where needed to deliver an agreed service, comply with applicable law or respond to a lawful request.

07

Overseas processing and disclosure

Some of our service providers operate internationally. Depending on their services and our account configurations, information may be processed, stored or accessed outside Australia. For more information about the providers we use, contact us at hello@weblorosae.com.au.

08

Information security

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information and restrict access to the systems used to deliver our services. Information may be stored in the third-party services listed above. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

09

Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to deliver services, maintain business records and meet applicable legal obligations. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it, subject to applicable retention requirements and the capabilities of the systems we use.

10

Access and correction

You can request access to personal information we hold about you or ask us to correct inaccurate information. Email hello@weblorosae.com.au. We may need to verify your identity before responding to certain requests.

11

Marketing

We may contact prospective businesses about our services where permitted by applicable Australian law. Marketing emails and SMS messages will be sent only where applicable consent requirements are met. We provide an opt-out method where required and respect unsubscribe requests.

You can ask us to stop sending marketing communications by emailing hello@weblorosae.com.au.

12

Websites we build for clients

WebLorosae may build websites for other businesses. Those businesses determine what information their own websites collect and should ensure that their privacy notices accurately describe their practices.

Where WebLorosae handles information on a client’s behalf under an agreed service, we use it to provide that service. This policy primarily describes WebLorosae’s own practices and does not replace a client’s privacy policy.

13

Questions and complaints

For questions or concerns about how we handle information, contact WebLorosae at hello@weblorosae.com.au. Please include details of your concern so we can investigate and respond.

Where applicable, unresolved privacy complaints may also be raised with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

14

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or information-handling practices change. The current version and effective date will be displayed on this page.
